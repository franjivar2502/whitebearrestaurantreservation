1. Privacy Policy

What we collect

  • When you book a table: your name, phone number, date and time, party size, occasion, seating preference, and notes and special requests.
  • When you answer an attendance request: whether you will attend or are cancelling.
  • When you post a review: the name you enter, your rating, your text, and any photo you upload. These are shown publicly on this website.
  • Automatically: like any website, our hosting provider receives your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, in technical logs used to run and protect the site. Your browser stores your language choice on your own device.

Notes may include allergy or dietary information if you choose to share it. Please share only what you want our team to know to prepare your visit.

How we use it

  • To hold and manage your reservation and assign a table.
  • To send you the messages about your reservation described in the Text Message Terms.
  • To contact you if something changes with your booking.
  • To publish your review and to respond to it.
  • To keep the website secure and prevent abuse (for example, fake bookings).
  • To comply with the law.

We do not sell your personal information, we do not share it for advertising, and we do not send marketing messages unless you separately ask to receive them.

Who we share it with

Only with service providers that run this website for us, and only for that purpose:

  • Render (website hosting, United States)
  • Supabase (database and photo storage)
  • Twilio (text message delivery)
  • Google Fonts (the site's typefaces are loaded from Google, which receives your IP address under its own privacy policy)

Mobile information: no mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties.

We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect the safety of our guests and staff, or as part of a sale or transfer of the restaurant.

Cookies and similar technologies

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies and does not track you across other websites. It stores only your language preference in your browser's local storage. Because we do not track you, we do not change our practices in response to "Do Not Track" browser signals.

How long we keep it

  • Reservation details: up to 2 months after the reservation date, then deleted.
  • Message delivery records: up to 2 months.
  • Reviews and photos: while they are published, or until you ask us to remove them.
  • Longer only if needed to resolve a dispute or required by law.

Your choices and rights

  • Ask us what information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by calling (518) 302-5235. We may need to verify your identity, and we will answer within 30 days.
  • Cancel a reservation using the link in your messages or by calling us.
  • Stop text messages at any time by replying STOP.
  • Ask us to remove your review or photo.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information, including encrypted (HTTPS) connections and restricting access to staff who need it. No system is perfectly secure. If a security breach affects your personal information, we will notify you as required by New York law.

Children

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Visitors from outside the United States

Your information is stored and processed in the United States.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here and update the effective date above.

3. Text Message Terms

  • What you receive: automated messages about the reservation you made: a booking confirmation, a request to confirm your attendance, and an alert about 15 minutes before your table is ready. That is usually up to 3 text messages per reservation, plus replies to your HELP or STOP requests.
  • Consent: by entering your mobile number and booking, you agree to receive these messages at that number for that reservation. They are informational, not marketing. Consent is not a condition of any purchase.
  • Opt out: reply STOP to any text at any time. You will receive one message confirming that you will not get further texts. Your reservation is not cancelled.
  • Help: reply HELP, or call (518) 302-5235.
  • Costs: message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies with your bookings.
  • Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
  • See the Privacy Policy for how we handle your number. We never share your mobile number or consent with third parties for marketing.

4. Review & Photo Policy

Honest reviews, good or bad

  • We publish positive and negative reviews. We do not remove, hide or delay a review because it is negative or has a low rating.
  • Reviews must describe your own real experience at White Bear Restaurant.
  • We do not pay for reviews or offer discounts, gifts or other rewards in exchange for them.
  • Owners, employees and their families do not post reviews on this site.

What we remove

We apply the same rules to every review, whatever its rating. We may remove or decline a review or photo that:

  • contains hate speech, harassment, threats or discrimination;
  • is sexually explicit or violent;
  • includes someone's personal information (such as a phone number or address);
  • is unrelated to the restaurant, is spam or advertising, or contains links;
  • appears fake, or was written by someone with a conflict of interest (for example, a competitor);
  • is illegal or infringes someone else's rights.

Your name and your photos

  • Your review is shown with the name you enter. You can use your first name only.
  • Only upload photos you took or have the right to share. Do not upload photos showing other people's faces without their permission, or children without their parent's permission.
  • Photos can contain hidden information such as the place where they were taken. Your phone's settings let you remove it before uploading.
  • By posting, you allow White Bear Restaurant to display your review and photo on this website, free of charge, including resizing or cropping the photo. You keep ownership of your photo. We will ask you before using it anywhere else, such as social media.
  • We may reply publicly to reviews.

Removing your review

To remove your own review or photo, or to report one that breaks these rules, call (518) 302-5235.

5. Accessibility Statement

We want everyone to be able to book a table with us. We aim for this website to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, level AA, and we keep improving it.

  • Form fields have labels, and the site can be used with a keyboard and a screen reader.
  • The site is available in English, Spanish and French.
  • The moving photos next to the booking form can be paused.

Known limitations: photos uploaded by guests may not have text descriptions.

Need help? If anything on this site is hard to use, call us at (518) 302-5235 during opening hours and we will take your reservation by phone, with the same availability and at no extra cost.
